# Spellbook — Weapon Enchantments & Related Craft

Field reference for writers. Pair every enchantment with the correct oil (see `spirit_combat_guide.md`). Oil alone grazes; enchantment alone fails or adapts against you. Wrong tier on the wrong entity wastes the hour or enrages it.

---

## Weapon Enchantments

### Tier I — Flicker
**What it looks like:** A faint, unsteady light along the edge—candle-thin, easy to miss in daylight.

**What it does:** Makes a mundane blade *count* against thin spirits. Without it (and Moonbane oil), steel passes through Sorrowful dead and weak Wraiths as if they were fog. With it, cuts land and accumulate.

**Who can cast:** Any trained practitioner. Novices can learn it.

**Cost:** Negligible. Can be held indefinitely, or stored in chalk seals / linen ward strips for later use.

**Party use:** Elena can pre-charge spare blades overnight with Flicker seals so the whole party has a baseline without her holding focus on every weapon mid-road.

**Works on:** Sorrowful, weak Wraiths.  
**Fails against:** Wronged and above—light alone does not wound them meaningfully.

---

### Tier II — Soulfire Edge
**What it looks like:** Cold white flame on the steel. No heat. Shadows pull away from it.

**What it does:** Burns spirit-stuff that Flicker only annoys. Damage sticks on Wronged spirits and stronger Wraiths; wounds accumulate instead of closing between strikes. Still needs the matching oil (Specter or Soulbane depending on class).

**Who can cast:** Requires real training and concentration. Veterans (Elena, trained arcanists) can lay and hold it. Green casters struggle to maintain it under pressure.

**Cost:** While maintained, the wielder cannot sprint or block cleanly—attention is split between the edge and the fight. Drop focus, flame dies.

**Party use:** Elena can put Soulfire on the one or two blades that will meet the main threat. She cannot sustain it on everyone’s steel at once; Flicker seals cover the rest.

**Works on:** Wronged, stronger Wraiths.  
**Fails against:** Cursed, Umbra, Guilt-Eaters—Soulfire alone does not make damage stick on those.

---

### Tier III — Consecration
**What it looks like:** The blade goes briefly sacred—no showy fire; metal feels heavy, clean, wrong to unclean things. Some hear a hush around the edge.

**What it does:** Marks the weapon as holy for a short window. Damage finally *adheres* to Cursed dead, Umbra, and Guilt-Eaters (with the right oil and, for Umbra, the prep ritual). Without Consecration, those entities shrug or ignore wounds.

**Who can cast:** Priestly ordination or years of arcane/spirit study. Elena can attempt it; Cassius may know the theory and fail the endurance. Not a novice trick.

**Cost:** Drains the caster hard. Short duration. Do not plan to hold it through a long climb—use it at the door or the hour.

**Party use:** Elena’s high-end field craft. One consecrated edge for the knight or the Umbra hypothesis; everyone else on lesser charges.

**Works on:** Cursed, Umbra, Guilt-Eaters (plus Soulbane / censer as required).  
**Fails against:** Celestial-tier entities—Consecration is irrelevant to them.

---

### Tier IV — Stellarbound Strike
**What it looks like:** Celestial light through the weapon—star-cold, not holy.

**What it does:** Channels astronomical / celestial force. Only viable against ancient or celestial-tier entities, and only with Void Ichor and correct timing.

**Who can cast:** Scholars of astronomy, celestial magic, or rare ancient rites. Not Elena’s day kit; not Guild-street standard.

**Cost:** Physically damages the caster. Unusable below peak condition.

**Works on:** Ancient / celestial entities only.

## Seals (Stored Low Enchantment)

Elena’s chalk seals and linen ward strips can hold a **Flicker** charge for hours (overnight prep). They do not hold Soulfire or Consecration reliably—those need a living caster at the moment of use.

- **Flicker seal:** slap on blade before contact; light wakes; no mid-fight focus from Elena.
- **Soulfire / Consecration:** cast live, on the weapons that matter, when the threat is named.

---

## Beast Oils (Flesh, Not Spirit)

Separate kit from spirit oils. For mad animals and woodland predators on the approach road.

| Oil | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Bleed oil** | Opens and keeps wounds bleeding on tough hides |
| **Beastbane** | Cuts deeper into animal muscle/hide; ordinary predators drop faster |
| **Iron-rub** | Cold-iron film; bites “wrong” / curse-touched beasts; little effect on common animals |
| **Venom-draw** | On the cut, pulls/neutralizes venom so poison doesn’t finish the fight after |

Enchantments above are spirit-facing. Beasts die to steel + beast oils; Flicker does not make a wolf special—it only lights the blade.

## Quick Pairing Cheat Sheet

| Threat | Minimum enchantment | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Sorrowful / weak Wraith | Flicker | + Moonbane |
| Wronged / strong Wraith | Soulfire Edge | + Specter or Soulbane |
| Cursed / Umbra / Guilt-Eater | Consecration | + Soulbane (+ censer / ritual as needed) |
| Celestial | Stellarbound Strike | Party does not pack this for Gravedawn |
| Mad beasts on the road | None required | Bleed / Beastbane / Iron-rub / Venom-draw |
