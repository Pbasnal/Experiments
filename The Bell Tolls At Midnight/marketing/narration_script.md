# Marketing Narration — The Bell Tolls at Midnight

**Tone:** Dark fantasy teaser. Bleak, mythic, invitation-only. Inspired by the atmosphere of Dark Souls / Elden Ring — doom, beauty, and a town that has already lost too much.

**Hard rule:** Do not hint at hidden secrets (true history of the tower, what sleeps beneath, identities behind the public legend, or any late-story revelations). Sell the surface mystery: a dying coastal town, a cursed tower, a solstice midnight, and five adventurers with one chance.

**Runtime target:** ~60–75 seconds

---

## Full Script

In Gravedawn, the fog comes early.

Salt rings every doorstep.
Empty windows watch the street.
And on the hill, the tower waits —
patient as an executioner.

People vanish between dusk and dawn.
Doors found open.
Lamps still burning.
No bodies left behind.

Priests bless the thresholds.
Families pack what they can carry.
The rest stay, redraw their wards each morning,
and try not to look at the hill.

Winter’s longest night is nearly here.
Five adventurers ride toward a town that has stopped believing in hope —
and toward a curse that has outlived every sword, every prayer,
every brave fool who climbed to meet it.

When the bell tolls at midnight…
will they cleanse what waits in the stone —
or become another name scratched from the ledger?

**The Bell Tolls at Midnight.**

A dark fantasy story.
Coming soon —
read it when the tale is fully told.

## Spoiler blacklist (never use in ad copy or image prompts)

- Anything beneath the tower / prison / void entities
- True identity or innocence of the “witch”
- Romantic history tied to the bell
- Church guilt / cover-up specifics beyond “a witch died there”
- Named late-game revelations from the PDF lore bible
