# Image prompts used for the teaser

All prompts ask for Dark Souls / Elden Ring concept-art mood. None mention underground prisons, void entities, the witch’s true identity, or Alric.

1. **Gravedawn fog** — crumbling coastal fishing town at dusk, leaning gray houses, thick fog from a cold harbor, desaturated slate palette.
2. **Salt circles** — white salt circle on a weathered doorstep, chalk wards, hollow windows of abandoned houses.
3. **Tower on the hill** — lonely stone observatory tower silhouetted above the foggy town; patient, wrong stillness.
4. **Empty door / lamp** — door ajar at night, oil lamp still burning inside, empty street; disappearance implied, no corpse.
5. **Priests and exodus** — soot-gray priests blessing thresholds while families pack carts under winter fog.
6. **Adventurers on the road** — cart of weary travelers on a misty forest winter road.
7. **Party faces the tower** — five small figures at the foot of the hill path, tower looming in moonlight.
8. **Midnight bell** — ancient bronze bell in a stone belfry at midnight, frost on metal, about to toll.
9. **Title card** — composited typography over a darkened tower plate (readable text; not AI lettering).
