In Gravedawn, the fog comes early.

Salt rings every doorstep. Empty windows watch the street. And on the hill, the tower waits — patient as an executioner.

People vanish between dusk and dawn. Doors found open. Lamps still burning. No bodies left behind.

Priests bless the thresholds. Families pack what they can carry. The rest stay, redraw their wards each morning, and try not to look at the hill.

Winter’s longest night is nearly here. Five adventurers ride toward a town that has stopped believing in hope — and toward a curse that has outlived every sword, every prayer, every brave fool who climbed to meet it.

When the bell tolls at midnight… will they cleanse what waits in the stone — or become another name scratched from the ledger?
