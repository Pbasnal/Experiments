Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 July 2026 · V0 validation launch

What we collect

  • Creators / admins: Google account email, display name, and session cookies to keep you signed in.
  • Readers: an anonymous reader_id cookie (random UUID, HttpOnly, 1 year) used only for product analytics — no account link in V0.
  • Usage events: series/chapter view events (series id, optional chapter id, referrer class, time). No advertising IDs.
  • Uploads: comic images and series covers you choose to publish.

How we use data

Operate the service, show schedule/share experiences, measure whether readers return after skips, and respond to abuse reports. We do not sell personal data.

Cookies

Session cookie for signed-in users; reader_id for anonymous analytics. Essential for the product; no third-party ad cookies in V0.
